# CPG trade promotion and deduction benchmarks (sourced)

Compiled 2026-10-24 for the General Mills trade-promotion accounting simulation.

Conventions:
- **Quote** is verbatim unless marked *(paraphrase)*. Every SEC quote was pulled from the filing text downloaded from EDGAR. Web quotes were checked on the fetched page unless the row says otherwise.
- **Confidence:** high = primary source, verified; medium = credible secondary, or primary but dated or narrow; low = unverified or anonymous.
- **Type:** independent (filing, survey, consultancy, press) or vendor (a firm that sells TPM or deduction software/services).
- None of these thresholds are GAAP. Any tolerance the simulation uses is company policy (rule 02).
